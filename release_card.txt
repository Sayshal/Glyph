## New Features

- Move Token moves a token to another level when the destination names a level.
- New Move Tile action moves a Tile so its center sits on a point, with an optional timed slide.
- Polygon Regions have vertex handles for moving, adding and removing points.
- The Active Tiles importer converts Move Token actions aimed at a Tile into Move Tile.
